How it works

  • Each round adds one glowing tile to the sequence.
  • Watch carefully, then replay the signal in the same order.
  • One mistake ends the run. Your best streak stays on this device.
  • Take a short focus break, then try again.

No account, backend, analytics, external assets, or paid dependencies. Data is stored in local browser storage only.
